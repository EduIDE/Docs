# Student Overview

EduIDE is a full IDE in your browser.
You open a link, and your IDE is ready: compiler, language server, debugger, terminal and editor settings for your language.

Terms such as template, session and workspace are defined in the [glossary](/glossary).

## Why EduIDE

- **Nothing to install.** Everything runs in the browser tab.
- **The same setup for everyone.** Your tutor sees what you see, and "it works on my machine" does not happen.
- **One click from your course.** If your course uses Artemis, the exercise opens in the IDE and your repository is already there.

## What you get

- A real IDE (built on Eclipse Theia) in seven languages: Java 17, Java 25, Python, JavaScript, C, Rust and Go.
- A terminal, debugger, linting and a spell checker (English and German).
- A saved workspace or a temporary session, depending on how you start. See [Saved Workspaces and Temporary Sessions](./getting-started/workspaces.md).

## Limits

- You need a network connection. There is no offline mode.
- A session stops after a period without input (60 minutes by default). See [When Your Session Ends](./getting-started/session-timeout.md).
- Temporary sessions lose their files when the session ends. Push your work to keep it.
- You cannot install your own system tools or use unrestricted machine access.
- Your installation decides which languages you see and how many sessions you can run at the same time.

:::info Your installation
EduIDE is not tied to one institution. Your university or course runs its own installation, so the address, the login and the list of templates differ. TUM is one example. Ask your tutor for the address.
:::

## How do I start?

| Your situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Your course uses Artemis | [Start from Artemis](./getting-started/start-from-artemis.md) |
| Your tutor sent you a link to EduIDE | Open it, sign in, and the session starts. See [First Login](./getting-started/first-login.md) |
| You want to work with your own GitHub or GitLab repository | [Private Git Repositories](./learning/private-repositories.md) |
| You want to explore or practise | Open the landing page of your installation, sign in and pick a tile. See [First Start](./getting-started/first-start.md) |
| You want to continue earlier work | [Your Sessions](./getting-started/your-environments.md) |
