# Student Overview

Welcome to EduIDE. This guide helps you get coding quickly in your course workspace.

You do not need local setup. Everything runs in your browser.

:::info TUM-specific examples
Some steps and screenshots may reflect how EduIDE is currently used at TUM, for example with Artemis course flows. EduIDE itself is an independent product and can be implemented for students at any institution.
:::

## What this section covers

- logging in to the platform
- launching your workspace in one of seven languages
- launching from Artemis exercises
- navigating the IDE layout and the experience levels
- what happens when a session ends, and how to keep your files
- editing, compiling, and running code from the terminal
- submitting from the Artemis view and checking feedback
- reporting problems and asking questions
