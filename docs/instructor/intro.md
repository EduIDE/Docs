# Instructor Overview

This section is for instructors and course owners who want to evaluate whether EduIDE is a good fit for their teaching setup.

The focus is what kind of course benefits from EduIDE, what must be true before adoption makes sense, and which limitations you accept.

:::info TUM-specific evaluation context
Some examples may reflect how EduIDE is used at TUM. EduIDE is independent of one institution. Your university runs its own installation, so addresses, logins and the list of environments differ.
:::
