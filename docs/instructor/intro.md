# Instructor Overview

This section is for instructors and course owners who want to evaluate whether EduIDE is a good fit for their teaching setup.

The focus here should not be "how to use every feature." It should be "what kind of course benefits from EduIDE, what needs to be true before adoption makes sense, and what limitations need to be accepted."

## What lives here

- Which course setups fit EduIDE well
- What can realistically be evaluated during a pilot
- What cannot be judged fairly in a shallow demo
- Technical and organizational prerequisites
- Limitations and adoption risks
