# First Lab Checklist

## Weeks before

- Confirm the languages and editions your installation offers.
- Check that your Artemis includes pull request #14702 (not yet merged upstream). Without it, the exercise does not clone itself.
- Ask for your Artemis server and repository host to be on the list of trusted hosts.
- Decide: saved or temporary workspace, and the retention.
- Tell the platform team the date, the time and the number of students.

## A few days before

- Open your own exercise from Artemis as a student, change a file, push and check the result in Artemis.
- Do it on the room's network.
- Check the public status page of the installation.

## In the lab

- Ask students to start the IDE a few minutes early and not to reload while it starts.
- Say once: push before you leave, and a session stops after 60 minutes without input.
- Know the contact for platform problems, and have a plan if the platform is down.

## After the lab

- Collect problems and report them (see [Get Help](/student/learning/get-help)).
- Look at the common failure in the results. A failure many students share is usually an exercise problem.
